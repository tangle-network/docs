# Tangle Integration Hub

Your Tangle workspace gets more useful when it can see the tools you already
run your business on. This hub collects a plain-English guide for each
connection, including the account access, costs, and setup limits to check
before you start.

## How a connection works

1. **Check the provider's requirements.** Cloudbeds, PriceLabs, and Deepgram
   use API keys from your account. WhatsApp uses the in-app business phone
   setup instead. PriceLabs API access is paid, and its current Hospitality
   setup has a limitation described in the guide below.
2. **Open Integrations in your Hospitality workspace.** Business data
   providers appear under **Business integrations**. The separate
   **Connections** page manages messaging channels; it is not the API-key
   setup page.
3. **Connect and register the provider for business data.** Follow the
   provider's guide and check the connection status before relying on its
   answers. Deepgram needs a separate **Use for business data** step.

Message delivery and write actions follow the workspace's mode, roles, and
action-specific approval rules. Connecting a provider does not mean every
message or action waits for approval; check those controls before going live.

## Your keys stay yours

- Keys are stored encrypted in Tangle's integration vault and are not shown
  again by the workspace after you paste them.
- A provider key's permissions and Tangle's connector behavior are different.
  The provider decides what the key can access; Tangle limits the operations
  and fields its connector exposes. Choose the narrowest provider access
  that supports your intended setup.
- Tangle associates a connection with your workspace. That does not narrow
  the key's permissions at the provider. For example, leave Cloudbeds'
  organization-wide option off for a single-property setup.
- Disconnect in **Integrations** to stop using a connection in the workspace.
  Revoke or replace the key at the provider if you need to invalidate the
  credential itself. Follow the provider guide when reconnecting.

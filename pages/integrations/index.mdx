# Tangle Integration Hub

Your Tangle workspace gets dramatically more useful when it can see the tools
you already run your business on. This hub collects a short, plain-English
guide for each connection. Every guide follows the same shape: **you create a
key in the other tool, you paste it into your workspace, your agent starts
using it.** No developer needed, and each one takes about five minutes.

## How a connection works

1. **You create an access key inside the other tool** (Cloudbeds, PriceLabs,
   Deepgram…). The key belongs to your account, and you choose exactly what it
   is allowed to do.
2. **You paste that key into your workspace**, under
   **Setup → Connections**. Your workspace asks one or two follow-up questions
   (like which property the key is for) and takes it from there.
3. **Your agent starts using the connection** — answering questions from real
   reservations, checking today's arrivals, listening to voice notes. Anything
   that sends a message or charges money still waits for a person's approval.

## Your keys stay yours

- Keys are stored encrypted in Tangle's integration vault. They are never
  shown again after you paste them, not even to your own workspace members.
- Each key is scoped to one business and to the smallest set of permissions
  the integration needs — every guide lists them exactly.
- You can disconnect or rotate a key at any time, in the other tool or in
  **Setup → Connections**. Disconnecting stops the data flow immediately;
  your history stays.
- A connection only _reads_ your data (plus the few write actions each guide
  names). It never gives your agent permission to send messages or approve
  charges on its own.

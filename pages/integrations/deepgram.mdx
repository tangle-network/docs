# Connect Deepgram

People send voice notes — a guest asking about late checkout by WhatsApp, a
housekeeper reporting a room by voice while their hands are full. With
Deepgram connected, your agent **transcribes those voice notes and answers
them like any text message**.

You connect it by creating one API key inside Deepgram and pasting it into
your workspace.

**Time:** about 2 minutes. **You need:** a Deepgram account (the free tier is
enough to start).

## Step 1 — Create an API key in Deepgram

1. Sign in to the Deepgram console.
2. Open **API Keys** and create a new key.
3. Copy the key.

## Step 2 — Add the key to your workspace

1. Open your Tangle workspace (for example
   [hospitality.tangle.tools](https://hospitality.tangle.tools)) and go to
   **Setup → Connections**.
2. Choose **Deepgram** and paste the key.
3. Confirm. There are no extra questions — the connection starts working for
   every voice note your business line receives.

## Step 3 — Confirm it's working

Send a short voice note to your business line ("Testing, testing, one two
three"). The agent should reply as if you had typed it.

## Good to know

- Usage follows your own Deepgram plan — your agent simply uses your key.
- Only voice notes sent to your business line are transcribed. The agent
  never listens to anything else.
- The key is stored encrypted in Tangle's integration vault, never displayed
  again, and works only for this workspace.
- To cut the connection, remove Deepgram in **Setup → Connections** or delete
  the key in Deepgram.

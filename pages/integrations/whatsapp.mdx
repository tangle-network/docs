# WhatsApp business line

Your workspace can run a business number that enrolled **guests, staff, and
vendors** text. The agent handles messages according to each person's role
and the workspace's delivery settings. This setup happens in the workspace;
you do not paste a WhatsApp API key into the business integrations panel.

## Choose your number

**A Tangle-managed number** — select an available WhatsApp line from
**Business phone**. Availability depends on your workspace. If the section
says **Not available**, ask Tangle to enable it.

**Your own WhatsApp Business number** — authorize the business account through
Meta, select the number, then complete the separate manager-phone verification
below.

## Connect it in your workspace

1. Open your [Hospitality workspace](https://hospitality.tangle.tools) as an
   owner or manager and go to **Connections**.
2. If using your own number, open **WhatsApp Business**, choose
   **Connect WhatsApp Business**, then **Continue with Meta**. Complete Meta's
   authorization, choose the **Business account** and **WhatsApp number**, and
   select **Use this number**.
3. In **Business phone**, select your available WhatsApp line and choose
   **Verify my phone**. This step also applies to a Tangle-managed line.
4. From your own manager or owner handset, send the exact **TEST** text to
   the destination displayed in the workspace. Then reply to the code you
   receive. Follow the displayed instructions; do not substitute a generic
   hello or send the code from another phone.
5. Select **I replied to the code** if the status has not refreshed. After
   verification succeeds, choose **Connect line**. If the challenge expired,
   choose **Get a new code** and use the new TEST text.
6. When the line shows **Connected**, add the intended guests, staff, and
   vendors from **People** and follow their enrollment instructions.

The TEST/reply challenge proves control of your manager handset. It is
separate from authorizing a WhatsApp Business account and number through
Meta; completing one does not complete the other.

## Good to know

- **Connections** contains business phone setup. **Integrations** is the
  separate page for Cloudbeds, PriceLabs, and Deepgram.
- Replies and outbound requests follow the workspace's mode, roles, approval
  rules, and recipient consent. Do not assume every guest reply requires a
  manager's approval.
- Follow the phone opt-in instructions shown for each enrolled person before
  expecting approved messages or reminders to reach them.
- Conversation and action records in the workspace help you check what was
  received, approved, or sent. Verify the status of a specific message rather
  than assuming that connecting a line proves delivery.

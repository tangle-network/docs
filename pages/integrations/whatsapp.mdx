# WhatsApp business line

WhatsApp is where your guests already are. Your workspace can run one business
number that your **guests, staff, and vendors** text — and your agent answers
on it, in each person's own language, with the permissions of the person
writing. You don't create keys in any developer portal for this one: the whole
flow runs inside your workspace.

## Choose your number

**A Tangle-managed number** — we provision and maintain the WhatsApp number
for you. Best when you want to start immediately or don't have a WhatsApp
Business number yet.

**Your own WhatsApp Business number** — you connect the number your customers
may already have saved. Best when you've advertised a number you don't want to
change.

## Connect it in your workspace

1. Open your Tangle workspace (for example
   [hospitality.tangle.tools](https://hospitality.tangle.tools)) and go to
   **Setup → Connections**.
2. Find the **Business phone** section and choose to add a WhatsApp line.
3. Follow the in-app steps for the path you chose. Connecting your own number
   includes a one-time **owner verification** — a code sent to the number's
   owner — so nobody can attach a number that isn't theirs.
4. When the line shows **Active**, send it a hello from your own phone.

The workspace knows who's writing by their enrolled number. Unknown numbers
get a friendly introduction, not access; each person sees only what their role
allows.

## Good to know

- Messages that go out under your business's name — vendor requests, guest
  replies — wait for a manager's approval while you're in approval mode.
  You choose the mode in **Setup**.
- Pausing the line stops every queued send instantly, from **Work**.
- Every conversation, approval, and receipt is visible in your workspace —
  WhatsApp is the channel, your workspace is the record.
